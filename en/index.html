SINCE 2016 — THAILAND

In Thailand, for 10 years

IPI (THAILAND) operates Napoli no Kama, a pizza brand born in Japan, here in Thailand. Since our founding in 2016, we have grown to 5 company-owned and franchise branches.

SCROLL

PHILOSOPHY

One pizza at a time, every day

In the restaurant business, staying open is harder than opening. We have been entrusted with the Napoli no Kama brand in Thailand and have kept our doors open for 10 years.

Rather than rapid expansion, we focus on baking and serving today's pizza properly. We believe that only this steady work earns the trust of our customers and of the owners who entrust us with their branches.
