Brand Management

Brand management

We operate Napoli no Kama, a pizza brand born in Japan, in Thailand

Under a master franchise agreement with Napoli no Kama in Japan, IPI manages the brand in Thailand. We opened our first branch in Asok, Bangkok in 2018, and have since kept the stone-oven pizza methods and quality standards developed in Japan alive in the Thai market.

Company-owned branch

At the company-owned branch beside our Pattaya head office, we put the brand's standards into practice ourselves

We opened this branch in 2022 when our head office moved to Pattaya. As well as being a business in its own right, it serves as the model branch that sets the standards for cooking, hygiene and service across our franchises.
